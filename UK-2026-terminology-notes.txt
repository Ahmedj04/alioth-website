ALIOTHDIGITAL — 2026 UK TERMINOLOGY & CONTENT NOTES

Primary customer-facing hierarchy:
WIN — Proposal & Bid Management
BUILD — Recruitment & Talent
GROW — Digital, Software & IT

UK procurement language to favour:
- bid / tender / tender opportunity
- Invitation to Tender (ITT)
- Selection Questionnaire (SQ)
- specification / statement of requirements
- evaluation criteria / award criteria
- response / tender response
- clarification questions
- compliance matrix
- bid/no-bid decision
- win themes
- evidence / case studies / social value
- submission readiness / final QA
- contracting authority / buyer / supplier
- lot / lots
- framework / dynamic market where relevant

Use “PQQ” only when referring to a legacy process or when a buyer/client specifically uses the term. The modern UK public-sector term is generally “Selection Questionnaire (SQ)”.

Tone:
- British English: organisation, programme, prioritise, fulfil, centre, licence (noun), practise (verb)
- Clear, confident and human.
- Avoid inflated claims such as “revolutionary”, “game-changing”, “AI-powered everything”.
- Prefer plain-English explanations and outcome-led headings.
- Keep “proposal” where it describes proposal development, but do not make “proposal” the only term for UK public procurement.

Design:
- Editorial typography + restrained premium palette.
- Warm off-white, charcoal, soft mineral green and muted sand.
- Real photography and original diagrams over generic AI/stock imagery.
- Motion should explain hierarchy or provide feedback, not decorate every element.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Mobile navigation is a full-screen, touch-friendly experience.
