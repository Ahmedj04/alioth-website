Frequently Asked Questions

IT Staffing FAQs.

Practical answers about scope, candidate availability, screening and hiring responsibilities.

Can you source candidates for niche technologies?

We can review niche or specialist requirements and confirm whether the role fits the available sourcing capability before work begins.

Do you technically test candidates?

Technical testing is not assumed unless specifically agreed. Screening can focus on CV evidence, experience and structured questions, while formal assessments can be incorporated where an appropriate process is available.

Can you support remote and hybrid roles?

Yes. Search criteria can reflect the working arrangement specified in the role brief.

Can you support both permanent and contract IT roles?

Yes, depending on the assignment and agreed recruitment model.
