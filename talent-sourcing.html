Frequently Asked Questions

Talent Sourcing FAQs.

Practical answers about scope, candidate availability, screening and hiring responsibilities.

Is talent sourcing the same as full recruitment?

No. Talent sourcing focuses primarily on identifying relevant potential candidates. Full recruitment can include wider screening, shortlisting, interview coordination and process support.

Will every sourced candidate be interested in the role?

Not necessarily. Sourcing identifies potentially relevant profiles; candidate interest, availability and suitability must still be confirmed through outreach and screening.

Can you build a talent pipeline for future roles?

Yes, where appropriate and subject to relevant data-handling and candidate-contact requirements.

Can we provide our own search criteria?

Yes. We can work from your existing role profile or help refine the criteria before the search begins.
