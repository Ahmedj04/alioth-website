Frequently Asked Questions

Contract Staffing FAQs.

Practical answers about scope, candidate availability, screening and hiring responsibilities.

What contract lengths can you support?

Requirements can vary from short assignments to longer project-based engagements. The practical options depend on the role, candidate market and agreed staffing model.

Can contract staff work remotely?

Yes, where the assignment allows it. The required working arrangement should be defined in the brief.

Do you handle payroll for contractors?

This depends on the staffing model and your actual operating arrangements. Payroll or employment services should only be stated where they are genuinely provided and contractually supported.

Can contract staffing be used for IT roles?

Yes. Technology and specialist project roles are common use cases, subject to the specific requirement and sourcing capability.
