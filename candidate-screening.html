Frequently Asked Questions

Candidate Screening FAQs.

Practical answers about scope, candidate availability, screening and hiring responsibilities.

Do you make the final hiring decision?

No. We provide screening support and structured candidate information. The employer remains responsible for interview, assessment and hiring decisions.

Can you use our existing screening questions?

Yes. We can work with an employer-provided screening framework or help structure questions around the agreed role requirements.

Do you carry out background checks?

Only if specifically agreed and supported by an appropriate process. Standard candidate screening should not be presented as formal background verification unless those checks are actually carried out.

Can screening be provided for candidates we have already sourced?

Yes. Candidate screening can be used as a standalone service for applicants or profiles already identified by your team.
