Important submission note

Keep authorised actions under your team's control.

Where portal access, account credentials, declarations or legally binding approvals are required, these remain under the control of your authorised team. Our role is to help organise and prepare the submission package and support the process within the agreed scope.

Why structured bid management matters

Make the deadline visible before it becomes urgent.

Tender deadlines are fixed, while information often arrives from multiple contributors. A clear plan reduces last-minute confusion, provides visibility of outstanding actions and gives the team more time for meaningful review before submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bid planning & submission FAQs.

Answers about bid timetables, contributors, submission arrangements and urgent tender support.

Can you manage the full bid timetable?

Yes. Depending on scope, we can establish and maintain a bid plan covering questions, contributors, evidence, reviews, approvals and submission readiness.

Can you coordinate with several internal contributors?

Yes. We can help organise inputs from different teams and maintain visibility of what is complete, outstanding or awaiting approval.

Can you submit the tender on our behalf?

Submission arrangements depend on the portal, buyer requirements, access permissions and the agreed scope. Where only an authorised representative can make declarations or submit, your organisation must complete those actions.

Can you support urgent tenders?

Potentially, subject to capacity, the volume of work and the time remaining. Sharing the tender pack and deadline early allows the scope to be assessed quickly.

Working towards a fixed tender deadline?

Map the workload before the deadline gets closer.

Share the documents and deadline so the bid workload, milestones and support required can be mapped.
