Why analyse before bidding?

Know whether the opportunity fits before you commit.

Tender submissions can demand significant time from commercial, operational and technical teams. A structured review helps uncover mandatory conditions, evidence gaps, resource demands and commercial risks before drafting begins.

What we analyse

Eight dimensions of the opportunity.

We turn a large tender pack into a structured view of what the buyer requires, what you can evidence and what needs attention.

Share a live tender →

01 · Eligibility and gateway checks

Mandatory accreditations, financial thresholds, insurance, policies and exclusions.

02 · Scope and strategic fit

Alignment with services, geography, contract size, capacity and growth priorities.

03 · Evaluation and weighting

Quality-price balance, scored questions, pass/fail tests and likely effort by section.

04 · Evidence readiness

Case studies, performance data, CVs, certifications, policies and supporting records.

05 · Delivery and mobilisation

Staffing, systems, timescales, dependencies and relevant TUPE or supply-chain considerations.

06 · Commercial considerations

Pricing structure, liabilities, contract terms and areas requiring specialist legal or financial advice.

07 · Competitive positioning

Credible differentiators, buyer priorities and initial win themes based on available evidence.

08 · Submission requirements

Portal instructions, word limits, attachments, clarification dates and final deadline.

Frequently asked questions

Tender analysis FAQs.

Clear answers on scope, timing, documents, confidentiality and next steps.

What is tender analysis?

A structured review of a live opportunity to assess requirements, fit, scoring, evidence, delivery demands and risks before or at the start of the bid process.

Is this the same as bid review?

No. Tender analysis examines the opportunity and your readiness, usually before drafting. Bid review assesses a response that has already been written.

Will you tell us whether to bid?

Yes. The report can provide a pursue, pursue with conditions, or do not pursue recommendation, supported by the evidence available. The final commercial decision remains yours.

What documents do you need?

Usually the tender notice, ITT or instructions, specification, evaluation criteria, pricing schedule, contract terms and appendices, plus relevant information about your organisation.

How long does the analysis take?

Turnaround depends on document volume, complexity and the deadline. We will confirm a realistic timescale after reviewing the material.

Can you guarantee that we will win?

No. Outcomes depend on the buyer's evaluation, competition, pricing, evidence and other factors. The service is designed to improve decision quality and preparation, not guarantee an award.

Can you help after the analysis?

Yes. Where appropriate, Alioth can support response planning, tender writing or bid review as a separate service.

How is confidential information handled?

We will explain actual storage, access and deletion practices and whether an NDA is available. We avoid generic security claims that are not backed by a defined process.

Which sectors do you support?

We support opportunities where Alioth has credible capability. Sector-specific claims should be based on evidenced experience.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is based on document volume, complexity, deadline and required outputs. A fixed quotation can be considered after an initial document review.
